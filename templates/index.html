How to explain this to anyone

  1. HTML is the furniture: the heading, the text box, the list, and the buttons.
  2. CSS is the paint and lighting: colors, spacing, and the rounded cards.
  3. Python is the notebook in the back: it saves what you type, marks things done, and deletes them.

When you press a button, the page sends a short message to Python. Python updates the notebook, then shows you a fresh page.
